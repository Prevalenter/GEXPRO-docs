Hardware Bill of Materials¶

This bill of materials is being verified item by item. Rows whose unit price or source is marked — are still being confirmed, and the table will be updated as parts are ordered.

The table lists the parts required to build one GX16 dexterous hand and one EX16 exoskeleton glove. Quantities are per device and follow the actuation scheme: 15 finger joints plus one palm lateral-swing joint for GX16, and four finger modules with four joints each for EX16.

Reference prices are manufacturer list prices in US dollars, excluding tax. Values follow the paper appendix, except for the controller, power supply, U2D2 and hub rows, which carry current store prices. The Source column links to the store page for the servo, controller and electronics parts; commodity items are marked as local supplier.

Where to buy¶

  • ROBOTIS is the manufacturer of the Dynamixel actuators, the OpenRB-150 controller, the U2D2 interface and the X3P cables; the Source column links to the corresponding store page. Regional distributors stock the same parts.
  • Each XL330 and XC330 unit ships with one Robot Cable-X3P 180 mm and the M2 bolts for the horn and frame, so the servo count already covers most of the cabling and small fasteners.
  • The 12 V supply is a free-voltage unit (100–260 V AC); the AC cord type depends on the region.
  • Bearings, screws, heat-set inserts, springs, straps and filament are standard hardware items and are best bought from a local distributor, where the exact type can be matched to the mechanical drawings.
  • Store prices are current selling prices. The controller, power supply, U2D2 and hub rows use store prices in this table; the remaining reference values follow the paper appendix.

Quantity notes¶

  • GX16 uses 15 XL330-M288-T servos at the finger joints and one metal-geared XC330-M288-T at the palm lateral-swing joint, which carries the additional torque of moving the palm and all four fingers.
  • EX16 uses 16 XL330-M077-T servos, four per finger module. The lower reduction ratio keeps transmission friction and back-driving resistance low while the operator moves their fingers.
  • A complete hand–glove system therefore uses 32 actuators: 15 XL330-M288-T, one XC330-M288-T and 16 XL330-M077-T. One spare of each of the two XL330 types brings the order to 34 actuators.
  • No separate cables are listed: every XL330 and XC330 ships with one Robot Cable-X3P 180 mm, and the power hub board set adds a 100 mm X3P and X4P cable.
